Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Because Ruchi Bites products are freshly prepared, perishable food items, our refund and cancellation policy is designed to be fair to both our customers and the handmade nature of our products. Please read this policy carefully before placing an order.

1. Order Cancellations

  • Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 2 hours of placing them, as long as packing has not begun. Contact us immediately via WhatsApp or phone to request a cancellation.
  • Once an order has been packed or handed over to our courier partner, it cannot be cancelled.

2. Damaged or Incorrect Items

We take great care in packing your order, but if you receive:

  • A damaged, leaking or spoiled package, or
  • The wrong product or wrong quantity,

please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with your order ID and clear photos/videos of the product and packaging. We will offer a free replacement or a full refund, at your choice, after verification.

3. Quality Issues

If a product doesn't meet our quality standards (e.g., taste, freshness), let us know within 24 hours of delivery with details and photos. Genuine quality issues will be resolved with a replacement or refund.

4. What Is Not Covered

  • Change of mind after the order has been packed/shipped.
  • Delays caused by incorrect address details provided at checkout.
  • Minor variations in shape, colour or texture, which are natural in handmade food products.
  • Products consumed or stored improperly after delivery (please store snacks in an airtight container in a cool, dry place).

5. Refund Method & Timeline

Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method via Razorpay within 5–7 business days. You'll receive an email/WhatsApp confirmation once the refund is initiated.

6. How to Request a Refund/Replacement

Write to us at hello@ruchibites.com or message us on WhatsApp with your order ID, issue description, and photos. Our team will respond within 24–48 hours.

7. Contact

For any questions about this policy, reach us at +91 90000 00000 or hello@ruchibites.com.

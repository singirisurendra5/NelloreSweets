Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Nellore Sweets ("we", "us", "our") operates this website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information when you visit or shop with us, in accordance with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact & shipping details: name, phone number, email address, delivery address, city, state and pincode, collected when you place an order or contact us.
  • Payment information: we do not store your card, UPI or bank details. Payments are processed securely by our payment partner, Razorpay, which is PCI-DSS compliant.
  • Order history: the products, quantities and packs you purchase, to fulfil and track your orders.
  • Technical data: basic browser/device information and cookies used to keep your cart working and improve site performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, pack and deliver your orders.
  • To send order confirmations and shipping updates via WhatsApp, email and/or SMS.
  • To respond to customer support queries and feedback.
  • To improve our products, website and customer experience.
  • To comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share limited data only with trusted service providers strictly for the purpose of fulfilling your order:

  • Razorpay — to process your payment securely.
  • Courier / logistics partners — to deliver your order, using only your name, address and phone number.
  • WhatsApp / email service providers — to send you order and shipping notifications.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure, access-controlled systems — to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your order and contact information for as long as necessary to fulfil orders, meet legal/tax requirements, and resolve disputes, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by writing to us at hello@nelloresweets.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Cookies

We use minimal cookies/local storage only to keep your shopping cart working across pages. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, reach us at hello@nelloresweets.com or call +91 90000 00000.
